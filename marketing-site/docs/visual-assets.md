# Visual assets

The public site uses two illustrative healthcare photographs created with the built-in ImageGen tool. They depict conceptual environments and do not identify real VIARA customer facilities or staff.

Project assets:
- `public/assets/scenes/radiology-care.webp`
- `public/assets/scenes/medical-team.webp`

The generated PNG originals were retained in the generating session. Project assets are WebP encodings at quality 86, without changes to the scenes or aspect ratio. Both images can be replaced from Admin → Brand & contact → Page photography.

Fonts are self-hosted, unmodified Tajawal Regular/Bold and Manrope Variable from the Google Fonts repository. Their OFL license files are retained in `public/fonts/`.

## Generation prompts

### Scene 1

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial hero photograph for VIARA, an Arabic and English healthcare technology website. Create a premium, natural-looking photograph of a contemporary radiology center: a realistic white MRI scanner in a bright calm examination suite, an Egyptian or Middle Eastern female radiologic technologist in muted teal scrubs and a male clinician in a white coat in relaxed professional conversation beside the scanner entrance. No patient on the scanner. Human warmth, attentive body language, candid atmosphere rather than looking at camera. Warm ivory walls, subtle emerald teal accents, pale oak cabinetry, natural daylight and soft realistic shadows. Horizontal 3:2 composition, medium-wide architectural photography, authentic skin texture and clothing, restrained premium healthcare editorial art direction. Keep people and scanner recognizable within a central crop suitable for a rounded website photo card. No text, no logos, no watermark, no readable medical records, no artificial sci-fi interfaces. This is an illustrative scene, not a real customer facility.

### Scene 2

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: supporting editorial photograph for VIARA healthcare technology website. Create an inviting and realistic photograph in a contemporary medical center: a Middle Eastern female doctor wearing a white coat over neutral teal clothing collaborates with a male medical administrator in a pale sand shirt while reviewing a tablet at a bright reception workstation. Both around their thirties or forties, calm friendly engaged expressions, candid working moment, no one looking at camera. Elegant warm ivory architecture, pale oak desk, a plant, soft daylight, subtle forest green accents, natural textures and soft depth of field, human centered premium editorial healthcare photography. Horizontal 3:2 composition with people centered, suitable for a 4:5 crop. No logos, no text, no watermark, no readable patient information on any screen, no exaggerated smiles. Clearly an illustrative professional environment.
